Recommendation: fund with conditions

Logistic regression with a threshold of 0.09. The model reorders the queue so likely cancers are read first; a pathologist reads and signs off every case.

  1. Re-validate on local de-identified samples before any patient-facing use.
  2. Pathologist sign-off on 100% of cases.
  3. Independent review at month six; pause if the standard-queue miss rate exceeds the locally validated rate.

What drives a priority flag?

Coefficients from the logistic regression model on standardized features. A positive weight pushes a biopsy toward the priority queue; a negative weight pushes it away. The strongest signals are worst texture, radius error and worst symmetry.

These are associations, not causes. They give pathologists something concrete to check, which k-NN cannot offer.
